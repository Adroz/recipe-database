# Microwave Sticky Date Pudding
* Serves: 1
* Prep time: 5 mins
* Cooking time: 2 mins

## Ingredients

### For the Caramel Sauce
* 1 tbsp unsalted butter
* 2 tbsp dark brown sugar
* 1 tbsp heavy cream
* 1 pinch salt

### For the Pudding Batter
* 2 Medjool dates, pitted
* 1 tbsp unsalted butter
* 2 tbsp heavy cream
* ¼ tsp baking soda
* 1 tbsp dark brown sugar
* ¼ cup (28g) all-purpose flour
* ⅛ tsp ground cinnamon
* 1 pinch salt

### For Serving
* Sour cream, vanilla ice cream, or whipped cream

## Method
1. **Make the Caramel Sauce:** Combine butter (`1 tbsp`), brown sugar (`2 tbsp`), heavy cream (`1 tbsp`), and salt (`pinch`) in a small microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring between intervals until melted and smooth. Set aside to thicken.
2. **Make the Pudding Batter:** Place dates (`2`), butter (`1 tbsp`), cream (`2 tbsp`), and baking soda (`¼ tsp`) into a separate 12-ounce microwave-safe bowl. Microwave for 30 to 60 seconds until dates soften. Mash the mixture thoroughly into a smooth paste.
3. Add flour (`¼ cup`), sugar (`1 tbsp`), cinnamon (`⅛ tsp`), and salt (`pinch`) to the date paste. Stir until just combined (do not overmix).
4. **Cook and Serve:** Microwave the batter for 45 to 60 seconds until the top is set. Pour the caramel sauce over the warm pudding. Top with a dollop of ice cream or cream.
