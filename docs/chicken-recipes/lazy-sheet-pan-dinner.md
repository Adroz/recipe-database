# Lazy Sheet Pan Dinner
* Serves: 4
* Time required: 60 mins
* Origin: Adapted from [Revi's Kitchen]([https://github.com/Adroz/recipe-database](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaJH6NGCqwq/))

## Ingredients
* 800g sweet potato
* 2 chicken breasts
* 1 cup frozen peas
* 1 tsp paprika
* 1 tsp onion powder
* salt and pepper
* butter for sweet potatoes

### Mustard Cream Sauce
* 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
* 2 tbsp dijon mustard
* 1 tbsp yellow mustard
* 1 tbsp honey
* Juice of 1/2 lemon
* 1/2 tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 tsp paprika
* 1 tbsp hot sauce
* Salt and pepper to taste


* [amount] [object], [preparation]
* e.g. 4 garlic cloves, minced

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200°C/400°F. Pepare a roasting dish with aluminium foil. Wash the sweet potatoes (`800g`)
1. Carefully poke sweet potatoes with a fork and roast for 35 minutes.
1. Meanwhile, mix the mustard cream sauce ingredients together in a bowl: Greek yogurt (`1/4 cup`), dijon mustard (`2 tbsp`), yellow mustard (`1 tbsp`), honey (`1 tbsp`), lemon juice (`1/2 lemon`), garlic powder (`1/2 tsp`), paprika (`1/2 tsp`), hot sauce (`1 tbsp`), and salt and pepper to taste.
1. Remove the sweet potatoes from the oven. Move them to the side of the pan and add the chicken (`2 breasts`) and peas (`1 cup`) to the same pan. Season the chicken and peas with paprika (`1 tsp`) and onion powder (`1 tsp`). Bake for an additional 25 minutes or until chicken reaches 74°C/165°F.
1. Plate by cutting open a sweet potato length-wise and adding a bit of butter to each half, using a fork to lightly mash up the butter with the potato. Add sliced chicken, roasted peas, and a drizzle of the mustard cream sauce.
